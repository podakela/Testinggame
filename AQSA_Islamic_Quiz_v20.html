About AQSA

AQSA — At-Tareeqatul Qadiriyyah Sunni Association — is a Muslim organisation working in Malawi and some areas of Mozambique to serve communities through Islamic education, Dawah, welfare, skills development and empowerment. Key Areas of Work Islamic Education: Supporting Madrasas, teachers and students and strengthening Islamic learning. Dawah: Promoting the teachings of the Qur’an and Sunnah and encouraging knowledge, good character and righteous living. Madrasa Development: Assessing educational needs and supporting improvements in Islamic education at the grassroots level. Modern Skills: Providing computer classes for young boys, helping the younger generation develop useful digital skills alongside Islamic education. Women’s Empowerment: Through initiatives such as the Mayi Fatima Tailoring Centre, providing women with practical vocational skills. Welfare & Charity: Supporting people facing hardship through food, essential supplies and other welfare initiatives. Community Development: Working with Shaykhs, teachers, parents, youth and local committees to strengthen communities and address their needs. Vision To build a knowledgeable, practising, skilled, self-reliant and united Muslim community through Islamic education, Dawah, welfare and sustainable development. AQSA strives to serve Islam by strengthening faith, educating minds, developing skills and empowering communities.
